About Our Rural Handicrafts

Indian Galleries is a Moradabad-based maker of handcrafted lighting, home décor and luxury kitchenware. Our rural handicrafts bring together artisans from Moradabad and rural India, and the gallery above showcases wooden handicrafts, basketry and weaving products shaped by hand using traditional techniques.

Craftsmanship

Each piece of rural handicrafts is made by hand by skilled artisans using traditional wood crafting, basketry and weaving methods. Because every item is handmade, small variations in shape, texture and finish are part of its character.

Design and Use

Wooden handicrafts, baskets and woven pieces suit living rooms, entryways, dining areas and gifting. Their natural, earthy look works well with both traditional Indian and modern interiors.
