About Our Handcrafted Table Lamps

Indian Galleries is a Moradabad-based maker of handcrafted lighting, home décor and luxury kitchenware. Our table lamps are made by skilled artisans, and the gallery above lets you browse the designs available in brass, metal and luxury finishes.

Craftsmanship

Each handcrafted table lamp is made by artisans in Moradabad, a city known for its metalware tradition, using brass and metal with carefully applied finishes.

Design and Use

Table lamps work well on bedside tables, consoles, desks and sideboards in homes, hotels and other interior projects. Their classic look suits both traditional and contemporary interiors.
