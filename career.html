Careers at Indian Galleries

Indian Galleries is a manufacturer of handcrafted lighting, home décor and luxury kitchenware based in Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh. Established in 2022, we work across design, production, quality control, accounts, purchasing and sales. If you care about craftsmanship and want to grow with a young company, we would like to hear from you.

Current Openings

Designer

Design luxury lighting, chandeliers, home décor, and handcrafted products for domestic and international markets.

Accountant

Manage financial operations, taxation, bookkeeping, and accounting for a growing home décor manufacturing company.

Quality Controller

Inspect handcrafted lighting, home décor, and luxury products to maintain exceptional quality standards.

Helper

Support various departments in daily operations and logistics.

Sales Manager

Lead and manage the sales team to drive growth and customer engagement.

Purchase Manager

Manage supplier relationships and oversee procurement of materials.

Working With Us

Our team brings together designers, quality controllers, accountants, purchase and sales professionals and support staff. Our products include handcrafted chandeliers, pendants, table lamps and floor lamps, as well as home décor and kitchenware, so every role involves a wide range of products and processes.
