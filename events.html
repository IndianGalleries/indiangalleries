Our Exhibition Journey

Indian Galleries participates in trade fairs, exhibitions and industry events to present its handcrafted lighting, home décor and decorative collections. These platforms provide opportunities to connect with buyers, designers, trade partners and organisations while sharing the craftsmanship behind our products.

IHGF Delhi Fair – Spring 2026

International Trade Fair

Indian Galleries showcased its handcrafted collections at IHGF Delhi Fair – Spring 2026, an international gifting and handicrafts exhibition. The participation provided an opportunity to present decorative lighting and luxury home décor collections to buyers and industry professionals. Our displayed creations brought together Indian craftsmanship and contemporary design for global interiors.

  • Hall 18 | Stall K-02/42
  • 14–18 February 2026
  • India Expo Centre & Mart, Greater Noida

FTF-I National Convention 2025

25 Years of Promoting Fair Trade in India

Fair Trade Movement

Indian Galleries participated in the FTF-I National Convention 2025, celebrating 25 years of promoting fair trade in India. Fair Trade Forum – India (FTF-I) works towards sustainable livelihoods, artisan empowerment and responsible trade practices.

The fair trade movement highlights the importance of fair wages, dignified working conditions and long-term opportunities for artisan communities. Our participation reflects our interest in ethical craftsmanship and responsible production.

Through shared learning and meaningful connections, the event offered an opportunity to engage with others working towards responsible trade and community development.

  • 12–14 December 2025
  • Fair Trade Forum India

Uttar Pradesh International Trade Show (UPITS 2026)

International Trade Exhibition | Greater Noida, India

Global Business • Indian Craftsmanship • Ethical Trade

Indian Galleries participated in UPITS 2026 – Uttar Pradesh International Trade Show, a platform connecting businesses, manufacturers, buyers and international trade partners. The exhibition provided an opportunity to showcase our handcrafted collections and connect with potential business partners.

Our collection featured decorative lighting, home décor and handcrafted products, reflecting Indian artistry, thoughtful design and craftsmanship for contemporary global spaces. The event also offered opportunities to discuss bulk requirements, customization and potential business collaborations.

  • 25–29 September 2026
  • India Expo Centre & Mart, Greater Noida
  • Hall No. 14 | Booth No. H14-05/27
  • Organized by the Government of Uttar Pradesh and India Exposition Mart Limited (IEML)
