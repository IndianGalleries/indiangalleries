What Our Certifications Mean

Below is a short explanation of each certification and registration shown above, and what it covers in general.

ISO 9001:2015

ISO 9001:2015 is an international standard for quality management systems. It focuses on consistent processes, customer satisfaction and continual improvement.

ZED Certification

ZED stands for Zero Defect Zero Effect. It is an Indian government scheme for MSMEs that encourages manufacturing with minimal defects and minimal impact on the environment.

MSME Registration

MSME registration recognises a business as a micro, small or medium enterprise under Indian government rules.

CE Marking

The CE mark indicates that a product meets the applicable European Union requirements for health, safety and environmental protection.

GST Registration

GST registration confirms that a business is registered for Goods and Services Tax in India.

Fair Trade Forum India

Fair Trade Forum India is a network of organisations that promotes fair trade practices in India. Our association with the network reflects our focus on fair, transparent and responsible craft practices.
