About Our Table Top Items

Indian Galleries is a Moradabad-based maker of handcrafted lighting, home décor and luxury kitchenware. Our table top items are made by skilled artisans, and the gallery above lets you browse the décor accents and statement pieces available.

Craftsmanship

Every handcrafted table top piece is made by artisans in Moradabad, a city known for its metalware tradition, using traditional metalworking techniques and finished with attention to detail.

Design and Use

Table top items suit console tables, sideboards, coffee tables and other living room surfaces in homes, hotels and styled interiors. They work well on their own or grouped with trays and vases.
